# Delete your account

You can permanently delete your MedTranscribeAi account from within the mobile app on **iOS** or **Android**. Deletion is immediate and **irreversible**.

> ⚠️ **Before you begin**
>
> -   Make sure you’re **logged in** on your device.
> -   Export any notes, transcripts, or receipts you want to keep.
> -   If you have an **App Store** or **Google Play** subscription, **delete does not cancel** billing. Cancel the subscription separately via Apple/Google to stop future charges.

---

## Steps (iOS & Android)

1. **Open the MedTranscribeAi app** and **log in** on your device.
2. Tap the **menu icon** (☰) at the **top‑left** of the screen to open the navigation drawer.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**
    > It appears **just above** the **Logout** button.
4. When the confirmation modal appears with **“Delete Account?”**, tap **Delete** (red button).
    > You can tap **Cancel** to back out without deleting.

That’s it — your account will be deleted and you’ll be logged out of the app.

---

## What happens next?

-   Your MedTranscribeAi **account and associated in‑app data** are scheduled for deletion.
-   This **does not cancel** Apple/Google subscriptions — cancel those in the App Store / Google Play.
-   Deleting your app account **does not alter records** in external systems (e.g., your EHR); those systems keep their own records.
-   We may retain minimal records as required by law or for security, in accordance with our privacy policy.

## Cancel your subscription (Apple / Google)

Deleting your MedTranscribeAi account **does not** cancel the store subscription.

-   **Apple App Store:** Settings → [your name] → **Subscriptions** → select MedTranscribeAi → **Cancel**
-   **Google Play:** Play Store → profile icon → **Payments & subscriptions** → **Subscriptions** → select MedTranscribeAi → **Cancel**

## FAQ

**Will deleting my account remove all my transcripts and notes?**  
Yes, your in‑app data is removed as part of the deletion process (subject to any legal retention requirements in our privacy policy).

**Can I undo this?**  
No. Account deletion is permanent.

**Do I get a refund after deleting?**  
Refunds for Apple/Google subscriptions are handled by **Apple** or **Google** per their policies. Deleting your account does not automatically generate a refund.
