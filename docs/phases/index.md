# Product Phases

📝 Phase 1 — Real‑time Transcription & ICD‑10
Dictate once; get a structured SOAP note and code suggestions in seconds—tuned to your style with Transcription Preferences.

🔗 Phase 2 — EHR Integration & Scheduling
Pull today’s schedule, pick a patient, and auto‑post notes/codes back to the chart.

✅ Phase 3 — Real‑time Claims Optimization
Models trained on CMS NCD/LCD + private‑payer directives (updated continuously) flag missing elements and prevent denials as you chart.

🛡️ Phase 4 — Compliance & FWA
Gentle, in‑workflow compliance nudges for clinicians and a dashboard for risk teams to catch patterns early.
